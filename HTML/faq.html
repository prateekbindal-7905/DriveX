Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about renting a car with DriveX.

How do I rent a car?

Renting a car is simple. Browse our cars page, choose your desired vehicle, select your dates on the booking page, fill in your details, and confirm the booking. You will receive a confirmation email with further instructions.

What documents are required?

To pick up the car, you must present a valid government-issued Driving License and a secondary form of identification (like an Aadhaar Card, Passport, or National ID) at the time of pickup. Both documents must be in the driver's name.

What is the security deposit?

A fully refundable security deposit of $500 is required for all rentals. This amount is held on your credit card at the time of booking and covers any potential incidental damages or late fees.

How is the deposit refunded?

The security deposit is automatically released back to your original payment method upon the safe return of the vehicle. It typically takes 3-5 business days for the funds to reflect in your account, depending on your bank.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes, you can cancel your booking up to 24 hours before your scheduled pickup time for a full refund. Cancellations made within 24 hours of pickup may be subject to a one-day rental cancellation fee.

What happens if I return the car late?

We offer a 1-hour grace period for returns. Beyond that, you will be charged an additional hourly rate or a full day's rate depending on the length of the delay. Please contact us if you know you will be running late.
